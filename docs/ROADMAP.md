# Roadmap

**Goal:** meaningful job-seeker traffic within 6 months, then monetize via company job posting fees.

**Constraints**
- Distribution is SEO-first plus a PricedIn brand page (no personal LinkedIn promotion).
- Automation budget: €50/month.
- Quality curation beats volume; roles at established companies build credibility.
- Owner capacity: 1-2 hrs on weekdays, up to 3 hrs on weekends.

**Phases** (see `CLAUDE.md` for the product priorities)
1. SEO and indexing, analytics
2. Content infrastructure: salary pages, category/location landing pages, blog
3. Newsletter (Buttondown)
4. Job aggregation (SerpAPI + Claude API classification)
